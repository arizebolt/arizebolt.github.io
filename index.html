01 — About

Infrastructure first,
models second.

I'm a Senior Data Engineer at Genpact, where I own the technical strategy for a Databricks Lakehouse platform that 10,000+ people across 40+ business domains depend on every day. My work sits at the layer most ML teams take for granted: the pipelines, the governance, the SLAs that decide whether a model ever sees production-quality data.

Before that, I spent a year in applied ML and research — building a RAG-based document assistant, running customer segmentation for retail personalization, and using neural architecture search and evolutionary algorithms to compress and optimize CNNs.

I like the seam between the two disciplines: systems that are correct, fast and cheap to run, in service of models that are worth running. I'm currently looking for engineering roles where both halves of that matter.
